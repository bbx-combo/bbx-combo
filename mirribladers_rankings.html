How rankings work

All MirriBladers events are ranked events. Your finishing position earns ranking points.

Solo events give the most points.
In duo and team events, the points earned by the team are shared equally between teammates.

For example, 1st place is worth 100 points. A solo winner gets all 100 points, while a 1st-place duo gets 50 points each.

Rankings are just for fun — they are a way to follow your progress, celebrate good results and keep climbing the leaderboard.

Rankings reset every quarter, giving everyone a fresh chance to climb. The next reset will be at the end of the year.
